    The Letters

    𐐨/iː/eat𐐲/ʌ/

    Many letters in the Deseret Alphabet have syllabic values (i.e. sounds represented when the letter is used by itself). The only truly useful syllabic value is 'thee' (as in the article 'the').

    There are two commonly used sounds in General American that were not accounted for in the original Deseret alphabet. These are the 'flapped t' (as in 'little' for many Americans) and the schwa sound in many unstressed syllables.

    If you have any qualms about the Deseret spelling system, refer to this document: Designing Deseret, University of Utah.

    Divinology

    An explanation of the positions of the site author.

    Tenets

    ○ Tenet 1: There are supernatural beings [La Salette (1846), Jesus (c. 30 CE), Fatima (1917), Medjugorje (1981–present), Garabandal (1961–1965), Cokeville (1986), etc.].

    ○ Tenet 2: A supernatural event can be proclaimed if there is no natural explanation and a future one is not forseeable.

    ○ Tenet 3: The three pillars of determining their trustworthiness are: moral character, predictive accuracy and retrospective accuracy.

    ○ Tenet 4: Many miraculous events have been misrepresented by Catholics [In the Motherly apparitions, there is very little reference to the Papacy and only as much as one would expect in a purely devotional context; this is a key point in assessing claims.]

    Texts

    La Salette — Apparition account overviewMelanie Calvat · September 19, 1846

    On September 18, the eve of the apparition, Melanie was watching her master’s cows alone. Maximin approached and said his master had sent him to watch the cows with hers. At first Melanie wanted to be alone, but eventually let him stay. They ate, played, and built a little structure they called “Paradise.”

    When Melanie woke, she saw a light brighter than the sun and then a beautiful Lady seated on the little mound. The Lady stood, crossed her arms, and said: “Come, my children, fear not, I am here to proclaim great news to you.”

    She spoke of the neglect of Sunday, the misuse of God’s name, spoiled harvests, famine, and the suffering of the faithful. She told Melanie and Maximin to pass the message on to God’s people. The Lady’s last words were a direct command: “And so, my children, you will pass this on to all my people.”
